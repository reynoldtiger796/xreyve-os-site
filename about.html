Connection status: TikTok login and automatic metrics synchronization are not yet enabled. This is a functional local-first review app, plus a public overview and legal information. We do not request passwords, session cookies, or verification codes.

How XREYVE OS works

Media opportunities are researched, original sources are checked for permission, relevant moments are reviewed, and edits are offered for human approval. Where metrics are available, their origin and observation date are recorded so estimated performance is never confused with confirmed account data.

For questions about this project, reach its maintainer through the official GitHub profile. Account connection and expanded analytics features are subject to platform permissions and further testing.
